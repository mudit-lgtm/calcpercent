Financial Percentage Calculations

Percentages come up constantly in personal finance and business. Here are the most common money-related percentage calculations in Australia:

GST (Goods and Services Tax) — Australia

Australia's GST rate is 10%. Here's how to work with it:

  • Add GST: Price + GST = Price × 1.10. Example: $200 + GST = $220
  • Find GST in inclusive price: GST = GST-inclusive price ÷ 11. Example: $220 ÷ 11 = $20 GST
  • Remove GST: Ex-GST price = GST-inclusive ÷ 1.10. Example: $220 ÷ 1.10 = $200

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate 10% of a dollar amount?
Move the decimal point one place to the left. Example: 10% of $250 = $25. Or use Module 1 above.
How do I calculate GST in Australia?
Add GST: multiply by 1.10. Remove GST from an inclusive price: divide by 1.10. Find GST in inclusive price: divide by 11.
How much is $450 as a percentage of $2,000?
(450 ÷ 2000) × 100 = 22.5%. Use Module 2 above to calculate instantly.
