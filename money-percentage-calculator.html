How to Calculate Percentages of Money Amounts

Percentage calculations come up constantly in everyday financial decisions — calculating a restaurant tip, working out GST on a purchase, figuring out what percentage of your income goes on rent, or calculating a sales commission. This free money percentage calculator handles all of these in seconds.

Common Use Cases

🛒

GST in Australia

Add 10% GST to a base price, or extract the GST component from a GST-inclusive total.

GST = Price ÷ 11
🍽️

Restaurant Tips

Calculate a tip as a percentage of a food or drinks bill and find the total to pay.

Tip = Bill × Tip% ÷ 100
💼

Sales Commission

Calculate your commission earnings from any sale amount at any commission rate.

Commission = Sale × Rate ÷ 100
🏠

Budget Percentages

Find what percentage rent, food, or any expense is of your total income or budget.

% = (Expense ÷ Income) × 100
📈

Investment Returns

Calculate the percentage return on an investment or the dollar value of a % gain.

Return = Principal × Rate ÷ 100
🏷️

Price Markups

Apply a markup percentage to a cost price to find the selling price and profit margin.

Price = Cost × (1 + Markup ÷ 100)

The Core Money Percentage Formulas

Find the dollar amount: Result ($) = Amount × (Percentage ÷ 100) Example: 20% of $150 = $150 × 0.20 = $30
Find the percentage: % = (Part Amount ÷ Total Amount) × 100 Example: $450 of $2,000 income = (450 ÷ 2000) × 100 = 22.5%
Add a percentage: New Amount = Original × (1 + % ÷ 100) Example: $200 + 10% GST = $200 × 1.10 = $220 Subtract a percentage: New Amount = Original × (1 − % ÷ 100) Example: $220 − 20% discount = $220 × 0.80 = $176

GST Calculations in Australia (AUD)

The Australian Goods and Services Tax (GST) is 10%. There are three common GST calculations you'll encounter:

Add GST to a base price: GST-inclusive = Base Price × 1.10 e.g. $300 base + GST = $300 × 1.10 = $330 Extract GST from a GST-inclusive price: GST = GST-inclusive ÷ 11 e.g. GST in $330 = $330 ÷ 11 = $30 (base = $300) Find base price from GST-inclusive: Base = GST-inclusive ÷ 1.10 e.g. Base of $330 GST-inclusive = $330 ÷ 1.10 = $300
🇦🇺

Australian note: Most retail prices displayed in Australia already include GST. The price on the shelf is the total you pay. When a business quotes a price "ex-GST," you need to add 10% to find the final price. Use the Add % to Amount tab above with 10% to calculate this instantly.

What Percentage of Your Income Are Your Expenses?

Personal budgeting often comes down to understanding what percentage of your income each expense represents. Financial planners commonly recommend the 50/30/20 rule: 50% of after-tax income on needs (housing, food, transport), 30% on wants, and 20% on savings. Use Calculator Tab 2 above — enter your expense amount and total income — to instantly see what percentage any expense is of your income.

For example, if your rent is $1,800/month and your take-home income is $5,200/month: ($1,800 ÷ $5,200) × 100 = 34.6% of income on rent. This is above the commonly suggested 30% threshold, which many Australians currently face given housing costs.

Calculating Money Percentage vs Percentage Off

There's a subtle distinction worth noting: "percentage of money" refers to finding a portion of a dollar amount (e.g. 20% of $150 = $30), while "percentage off" means reducing a price by a percentage (e.g. 20% off $150 = $120 final price). For shopping discounts and sale prices, use our dedicated discount calculator or percentage off calculator. For general dollar-amount percentage questions, use the calculators on this page.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about calculating percentages of dollar amounts, GST, tips, and commissions.

Multiply the dollar amount by the percentage divided by 100. Result = Amount × (% ÷ 100). Example: 20% of $150 = $150 × 0.20 = $30. Use Tab ① above for any values instantly.

To add 10% GST: multiply the base price by 1.10. Example: $300 base → $300 × 1.10 = $330 GST-inclusive. To extract GST from a GST-inclusive price: divide by 11. Example: $330 ÷ 11 = $30 GST (base = $300). Use the Add % to Amount tab above with 10%.

5% of $1,000,000 = $1,000,000 × 0.05 = $50,000. Use Tab ① — enter 5 in the percentage field and 1000000 in the amount field, or click the "5% of $1M" quick example in the sidebar.

Use Tab ②. Enter the expense as Amount A and your total income as Amount B. Formula: (Expense ÷ Income) × 100. Example: $450 rent out of $2,000 income = (450 ÷ 2000) × 100 = 22.5%.

Commission = Sale Amount × (Commission Rate ÷ 100). Example: 5% commission on a $12,000 sale = $12,000 × 0.05 = $600. Enter your commission rate in Tab ① with the sale amount as the dollar figure. See also: Sales Percentage Calculator.

Use the Add % to Amount tab. Enter the bill amount and tip percentage — the calculator shows both the tip amount and the total to pay. Example: 15% tip on $85 → tip = $12.75, total = $97.75.

Selling Price = Cost Price × (1 + Markup% ÷ 100). Example: 40% markup on $25 cost = $25 × 1.40 = $35 selling price. Profit = $35 − $25 = $10. Use Tab ③ with "Add" selected and enter your cost price and markup %.
