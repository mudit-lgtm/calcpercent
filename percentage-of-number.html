How to Find a Percentage of a Number

To find X% of a number, multiply the number by the decimal form of the percentage (percentage ÷ 100).

Result = Number × (Percentage ÷ 100)

Examples:

  • 35% of 200 = 200 × 0.35 = 70
  • 15% of $80 = $80 × 0.15 = $12
  • 72% of 25 = 25 × 0.72 = 18 (i.e., 18/25 = 72%)
💡 Tip: For score-to-percentage conversion (like 16 out of 20), divide the score by total and multiply by 100: (16÷20)×100 = 80%. Use our fraction to percentage converter.
