How to Find What Percentage One Number Is of Another

This is the most fundamental percentage question: "X is what percent of Y?" It's used for test scores, budget percentages, survey results, nutritional information, and countless everyday calculations.

The Formula

Percentage = (Part ÷ Whole) × 100

Common Examples

QuestionCalculationAnswer
