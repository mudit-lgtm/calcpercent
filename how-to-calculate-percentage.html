How to Calculate Percentage

A complete, beginner-friendly guide to calculating percentages — with step-by-step methods, mental math shortcuts, and real-world examples.

What Is a Percentage?

A percentage is a number expressed as a fraction of 100. The word comes from Latin per centum meaning "by the hundred." The symbol is %.

So 50% means 50 out of every 100, or one half. 25% means 25 out of every 100, or one quarter. 100% means the whole thing.

The 3 Types of Percentage Problems

Almost every percentage question falls into one of three types:

Type 1: Finding the Percentage ("X is what % of Y?")

Percentage = (Part ÷ Whole) × 100
  • Example: What percentage is 45 of 180?
  • Divide: 45 ÷ 180 = 0.25
  • Multiply by 100: 0.25 × 100 = 25%

Type 2: Finding the Part ("What is X% of Y?")

Part = Whole × (Percentage ÷ 100)
  • Example: What is 30% of 250?
  • Convert % to decimal: 30 ÷ 100 = 0.30
  • Multiply: 250 × 0.30 = 75

Type 3: Finding the Whole ("X is Y% of what?")

Whole = Part ÷ (Percentage ÷ 100)
  • Example: 15 is 25% of what number?
  • Convert % to decimal: 25 ÷ 100 = 0.25
  • Divide: 15 ÷ 0.25 = 60

How to Calculate Percentage Increase

% Increase = ((New − Old) ÷ Old) × 100

Example: Salary increased from $50,000 to $55,000 = ((55000 − 50000) ÷ 50000) × 100 = 10% increase

How to Calculate Percentage Decrease

% Decrease = ((Old − New) ÷ Old) × 100

Example: Price dropped from $80 to $60 = ((80 − 60) ÷ 80) × 100 = 25% decrease

Real-World Scenarios

Test score: You got 36 out of 45. What's your percentage? (36÷45)×100 = 80%

GST in Australia: A $220 item includes 10% GST. The GST = $220 ÷ 11 = $20 (base price = $200).

Sale discount: A $150 jacket is 30% off. Savings = $150 × 0.30 = $45. Sale price = $105.

Pay rise: Your pay went from $62,000 to $65,100. Increase = ((65100−62000)÷62000)×100 = 5%.

💡 Key principle: Every percentage problem can be solved by setting up the ratio Part / Whole = %/100 and solving for the unknown variable.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you calculate a percentage of a total?
Divide the part by the total and multiply by 100. Example: 30 out of 120 = (30/120) × 100 = 25%.
How do I work out a percentage without a calculator?
Use mental shortcuts: 10% = move decimal one place left. 5% = half of 10%. 25% = divide by 4. 50% = divide by 2. For example, 15% of 80: 10%=8, 5%=4, so 15%=12.
How do I calculate a percentage on a phone calculator?
Enter the number, press ×, enter the percentage, then press the % button (or ÷100 and =). Example: 20% of 150 → 150 × 20 % = 30.
