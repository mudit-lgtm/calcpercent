Our Mission

Percentage calculations are used by everyone — students checking exam scores, shoppers comparing discounts, business analysts measuring growth, scientists reporting errors. Yet most people either reach for a general-purpose calculator that shows no working, or struggle to remember the right formula.

We built CalcPercentage to fix that: one dedicated, beautifully clear website where you can solve any percentage problem in seconds, understand how the answer was reached, and never have to memorise a formula again.

Our Mission
Make percentage calculations instant, transparent, and free for every person on the planet — with clear step-by-step working so you learn as you calculate, not just get an answer.

How We Work

Every calculation on CalcPercentage follows the same transparent process: show the formula, substitute the numbers, compute each step, and display the answer — with a plain-language explanation alongside. We never show just a number.

  • ✅
    Mathematically Verified Formulas
    Every formula is cross-verified against academic, financial, and scientific standards. No shortcuts, no approximations unless explicitly stated.
  • 🔢
    Full Decimal Precision
    Results are displayed to sufficient decimal places to be useful in real-world applications — with rounding only applied to the final displayed output, not intermediate calculations.
  • ⚡
    Client-Side Calculation Only
    All calculations happen entirely in your browser using JavaScript — no servers, no data storage, no network requests. Your numbers never leave your device.
  • 🌐
    Works Offline
    Because all computation is client-side, the calculators continue to work even with a poor or intermittent connection once the page has loaded.
  • ♿
    Accessibility-First
    All tools use semantic HTML, proper ARIA labels, sufficient colour contrast, and keyboard-navigable interfaces — designed to work for every user.
🛡️
Our Accuracy Commitment
We take numerical accuracy seriously. If you ever find a calculation that appears to be incorrect or a formula that seems wrong, please let us know and we will investigate and correct it within 24 hours. Accuracy is the only thing we will never compromise on.

Privacy & No Tracking

We believe a calculator website should be a utility — not a data collection product. CalcPercentage is built with that principle baked in from day one.

  • 🔐
    No Personal Data Collected
    We do not ask for your name, email, location, or any personally identifiable information. None is required to use any tool on this site.
  • 🍪
    No Tracking Cookies
    We do not place advertising cookies or third-party tracking scripts on your device. No remarketing, no behavioural profiling.
  • 💾
    Your Numbers Stay on Your Device
    Every calculation is performed locally in your browser. The values you enter are never transmitted to any server.
  • 📊
    Anonymous Analytics Only
    We may use privacy-respecting, anonymised analytics to understand which tools are most used — no individual-level tracking, no cross-site data sharing.
