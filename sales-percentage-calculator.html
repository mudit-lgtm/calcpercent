Sales Percentage Calculations in Business

Sales percentages are essential for tracking performance, setting targets, and calculating commissions. These three calculators cover the most common scenarios in Australian business environments.

Sales Target Achievement

This tells you what percentage of your sales target you achieved. If your team hit $45,000 of a $50,000 target, that's 90% achievement. Most organisations consider 90%+ as on-track.

Sales Growth Rate

Used to measure quarter-over-quarter or year-over-year growth. Formula: ((Current − Previous) ÷ Previous) × 100. A growth rate of 20% year-over-year is considered strong for most industries.

Commission Calculation

Real estate agents in Australia typically earn 1.5–3% commission. Financial advisors may earn trail commissions of 0.2–0.5%. Car salespeople often earn 2–5% of the sale price. Use this calculator to quickly work out your earnings.

📊 Australian context: GST (10%) should generally be excluded from commission base calculations unless otherwise specified in your employment contract. Check with your accountant for tax implications.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate what percentage of my target I achieved?
(Actual Sales ÷ Target) × 100. For example, $45,000 actual vs $50,000 target = 90%.
What is a typical real estate commission in Australia?
Real estate commission rates in Australia vary by state but are typically 1.5% to 3.5% of the sale price. For a $600,000 property at 2%: commission = $600,000 × 0.02 = $12,000.
