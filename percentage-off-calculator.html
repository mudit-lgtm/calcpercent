How to Calculate Percentage Off

Whether you're shopping at Kmart, JB Hi-Fi, or any online retailer, knowing how to calculate percentage off helps you compare deals and understand your true savings.

The Formula

Sale Price = Original Price × (1 − Discount% ÷ 100)
Savings = Original Price × (Discount% ÷ 100)

Quick Mental Math

To calculate 20% off in your head: find 10% (move decimal left), then double it. Subtract from original. For 25% off: divide by 4, then subtract.

🛍️ Shopping Tip: Always compare the sale price, not just the discount percentage. A 50% off item that was overpriced may still be more expensive than a competing product at full price.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate 20% off?
Multiply the price by 0.80 (1 − 20/100). Example: 20% off $75 = $75 × 0.80 = $60. Or: find 20% = $75 × 0.20 = $15, then $75 − $15 = $60.
How do I find what % off a price is?
Use: Discount % = ((Original − Sale) ÷ Original) × 100. Use the "What % Off?" tab above.
