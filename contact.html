❓ Quick FAQs
Is CalcPercentage free to use?+
Yes — 100% free, forever. No sign-up, no subscription, no hidden fees. All calculators are and always will be free.
Can I suggest a new calculator?+
Absolutely. Use the form above or email ideas@calcpercentage.app. We love good feature suggestions and have built tools based on user requests.
Why is my calculation wrong?+
It could be a real bug — please report it using the Bug Report option above with the values you entered. Or see the formula guide — sometimes the issue is which formula applies to your specific problem.
Do you offer an API?+
Not currently. If you'd like a percentage calculation API, email us at ideas@calcpercentage.app — if there's enough interest we'll build one.
