How to Convert a Fraction to a Percentage

Step 1: Divide numerator by denominator
Step 2: Multiply by 100
Step 3: Add the % symbol

Example: Convert 18/25 to a percentage:
18 ÷ 25 = 0.72 → 0.72 × 100 = 72%

💡 School tip: For test scores, divide your score by the total marks and multiply by 100. Got 27 out of 40? 27 ÷ 40 × 100 = 67.5%.
