1. Basic Percentage Formula

Percentage = (Part ÷ Whole) × 100

This formula answers: "What percentage is X of Y?"

Example: What percentage is 30 of 150?
(30 ÷ 150) × 100 = 20%

→ Use the Percentage Calculator

2. Percentage of a Number

Result = Number × (Percentage ÷ 100)

This formula answers: "What is X% of Y?"

Example: What is 25% of 80?
80 × (25 ÷ 100) = 80 × 0.25 = 20

→ Use the Percentage of a Number Calculator

3. Percentage Increase Formula

% Increase = ((New Value − Original Value) ÷ Original Value) × 100

Used when a value grows. The result is always positive for true increases.

Example: From $50,000 to $57,500:
((57500 − 50000) ÷ 50000) × 100 = 15% increase

→ Use the Percentage Increase Calculator

4. Percentage Decrease Formula

% Decrease = ((Original Value − New Value) ÷ Original Value) × 100

Used when a value drops. Result is expressed as a positive percentage.

Example: From 100 to 75:
((100 − 75) ÷ 100) × 100 = 25% decrease

→ Use the Percentage Decrease Calculator

5. Percentage Change Formula

% Change = ((New − Old) ÷ |Old|) × 100

Covers both increase and decrease. Positive = increase, negative = decrease.

→ Use the Percentage Change Calculator

6. Percentage Difference Formula

% Difference = |V1 − V2| ÷ ((V1 + V2) ÷ 2) × 100

Symmetric: order of V1 and V2 doesn't matter. Uses average as reference.

7. Percent Error Formula

% Error = |Experimental − Theoretical| ÷ |Theoretical| × 100%

Used in science labs to measure accuracy of experimental results.

8. Discount Formula (Percentage Off)

Sale Price = Original × (1 − Discount% ÷ 100)
Savings = Original × (Discount% ÷ 100)

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the basic percentage formula?
Percentage = (Part / Whole) × 100
How do I find the whole if I know the percentage and part?
Whole = Part ÷ (Percentage / 100). Example: 15 is 30% of what? 15 ÷ 0.30 = 50.
How do I find the part if I know the percentage and whole?
Part = Whole × (Percentage / 100). Example: 25% of 80 = 80 × 0.25 = 20.
