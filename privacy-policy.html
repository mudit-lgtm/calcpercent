This Privacy Policy explains how CalcPercentage ("we", "us", "our"), operating at calcpercentage.vercel.app, collects, uses, and protects information when you use our website and free calculator tools. By using CalcPercentage, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1Who We Are

CalcPercentage is a free online percentage calculator website available at calcpercentage.vercel.app. We provide free, browser-based tools for calculating percentages, percentage increases, decreases, changes, discounts, and other mathematical operations involving percentages.

CalcPercentage is operated as a standalone web utility. For any privacy-related enquiries, please contact us using the details in Section 12.

The Key Point
The numbers you enter into any calculator on this site — prices, percentages, salaries, test scores, whatever they are — are processed entirely within your own browser using JavaScript. They are never transmitted to CalcPercentage servers, never stored in a database, and never associated with your identity in any way.

3How Our Calculators Work

Every calculator on CalcPercentage — from the basic percentage calculator to the discount calculator, percentage increase tool, and all others — operates entirely client-side. This is a deliberate architectural choice made for both performance and privacy.

  • Client-side only: All calculation logic runs as JavaScript in your browser. No data is sent to any server to produce a result.
  • No API calls: Our calculators do not make network requests to process your inputs. The calculation happens instantly, locally.
  • No local storage of sensitive data: We do not write your calculation inputs to localStorage, sessionStorage, or any browser storage mechanism.
  • Works offline: Because all computation is local, the calculators continue to work correctly after the page loads, even without a network connection.
  • No login required: Access to every tool is immediate and anonymous — no account, no cookies required to function.
ℹ️ Technical Note

When you load a page on CalcPercentage, your browser makes a standard HTTP request to our hosting servers (Vercel) to download the HTML, CSS, and JavaScript files. This is normal web browsing behaviour and is identical to visiting any other website. The page files themselves contain no personal data about you.

4Cookies

A cookie is a small text file stored on your device by a website. Here is our complete and honest cookie usage:

Cookie Category Used? Purpose
Strictly Necessary Possibly Our hosting provider (Vercel) may set minimal session-level technical cookies. These are required for the site to function and contain no personal data.
Performance / Analytics Minimal / Optional Only if we use a privacy-respecting analytics tool. No individual-level tracking. Aggregated data only.
Functionality No We do not use cookies to remember preferences, logins, or personalisation settings.
Advertising / Targeting Never We place absolutely no advertising, remarketing, or behavioural targeting cookies. No Google Ads, Meta Pixel, or similar trackers.
Social Media Trackers Never No Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, or other social media tracking pixels are embedded on this site.

You can configure your browser to block, delete, or alert you about cookies at any time. Blocking all cookies on CalcPercentage will have no effect on calculator functionality — everything still works perfectly.

To manage cookies in your browser: Chrome → Settings → Privacy → Cookies · Firefox → Settings → Privacy → Cookies · Safari → Settings → Privacy · Edge → Settings → Cookies.

5Analytics

We may use analytics tools to understand how CalcPercentage is used in aggregate — for example, which calculators are most popular, which pages have high bounce rates, and how users navigate between tools. This helps us improve the site.

Our analytics approach follows these strict criteria:

  • No individual tracking: We do not track individual users across sessions, pages, or devices.
  • No cross-site tracking: We do not participate in any advertising network or cross-site tracking system.
  • Aggregated data only: Analytics data is used in aggregate form only — "1,000 people used the discount calculator today," not "User X used the discount calculator."
  • IP anonymisation: If any analytics tool processes IP addresses, anonymisation is applied before storage.
  • No third-party advertising data: Analytics data is never shared with advertising platforms or data brokers.
ℹ️ If We Use Google Analytics

If Google Analytics is used, it is configured with IP anonymisation enabled, advertising features disabled, and no remarketing lists. You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking across all websites using the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

7Data Retention

Because we collect almost no personal data, our retention policy is straightforward:

  • Calculator inputs: Never stored. Exist only in your browser's memory during your session and are discarded when you close the tab.
  • Anonymised analytics: Retained in aggregate form for up to 26 months for trend analysis, then deleted. No individual data is retained.
  • Contact emails: If you email us, your email address and message are retained only as long as necessary to respond and resolve your query — typically no longer than 12 months.
  • Server logs (Vercel): Standard server access logs are retained by Vercel per their own retention policy (typically 30 days). We do not control this data.

We do not maintain any user database, account system, or persistent record of individual visitors to CalcPercentage.

10Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect information against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. Specific measures include:

  • HTTPS everywhere: All connections to CalcPercentage are encrypted using TLS (HTTPS). The site does not serve content over plain HTTP.
  • Minimal data surface: The best security is not collecting data in the first place. Since calculator inputs are never transmitted, there is nothing to breach.
  • Hosted on Vercel: Our infrastructure is managed by Vercel, which maintains its own comprehensive security programme including DDoS protection, edge caching, and infrastructure security.
  • No database of user data: We operate no user database that could be compromised in a data breach.
  • Regular reviews: We periodically review third-party dependencies and scripts for security vulnerabilities.

No method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect any information, we cannot guarantee absolute security. In the unlikely event of a data breach affecting personal data, we will notify affected individuals and relevant authorities in accordance with applicable law.
