How to Calculate a Percentage

A percentage is a way of expressing a number as a fraction of 100. The word comes from the Latin per centum, meaning "by the hundred." Percentages are used everywhere — from shopping discounts and exam scores to financial growth rates and scientific measurements.

The Basic Percentage Formula

Percentage (%) = (Part ÷ Whole) × 100

For example, if you scored 45 out of 60 on a test: (45 ÷ 60) × 100 = 75%. This formula answers the question "what percentage is X of Y?"

Three Types of Percentage Questions

  1. Finding the percentage: What % is 30 of 200? → (30 ÷ 200) × 100 = 15%
  2. Finding the part: What is 25% of 80? → (25 ÷ 100) × 80 = 20
  3. Finding the whole: 15 is 30% of what? → 15 ÷ (30 ÷ 100) = 50

Quick Mental Math Tips

  • 10% — Move the decimal point one place to the left (e.g., 10% of 250 = 25)
  • 5% — Half of 10% (e.g., 5% of 250 = 12.5)
  • 25% — Divide by 4 (e.g., 25% of 200 = 50)
  • 50% — Divide by 2 (e.g., 50% of 180 = 90)
  • 1% — Move the decimal point two places left (e.g., 1% of 500 = 5)
  • 15% — Add 10% + 5% (e.g., 15% of 80 = 8 + 4 = 12)

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate a percentage?
Divide the part by the whole and multiply by 100. Formula: Percentage = (Part / Whole) × 100. For example, 25 out of 200 = (25/200) × 100 = 12.5%.
How do I find what percentage one number is of another?
Divide the first number by the second number, then multiply by 100. Example: What percentage is 30 of 150? (30 ÷ 150) × 100 = 20%.
How to calculate percentage of a number?
Multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. Example: 20% of 80 = (20 × 80) ÷ 100 = 16.
What is the percentage formula?
The basic percentage formula is: Percentage (%) = (Value / Total Value) × 100. There are three variations: finding the percentage, finding the part, and finding the whole.
